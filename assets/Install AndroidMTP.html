Welcome to AndroidMTP

Your Android files, on your Mac. No Homebrew or Terminal commands needed.

  1. Drag AndroidMTP onto Applications in the installer window.
  2. Open Applications → AndroidMTP.
  3. Connect your unlocked phone and choose USB File transfer.

You can eject the AndroidMTP installer after copying the app.

First opening: This community preview has not been notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it and you choose to trust this download, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find AndroidMTP, and choose Open Anyway. This is a one-time approval for this app. Apple’s instructions.

Apple Silicon Mac · macOS 13 or newer. USB debugging is not required.
